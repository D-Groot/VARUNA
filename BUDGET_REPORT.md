## 2. Hardware
The initial prototype is software-first. Sensor nodes, flow meters, pressure sensors, water-quality instruments, installation, calibration, and field connectivity are excluded from the above estimate. Obtain quotations before adding hardware costs.

## 3. Assumptions
- Team members use existing laptops and internet access.
- Open-source tools are used where suitable.
- The initial demo uses synthetic data.
- No paid government API access or field deployment is assumed.

## 4. Future Deployment Costs
A real deployment may require sensor procurement, installation, calibration, connectivity, maintenance, training, security, and authorized integration. These costs require a site-specific bill of materials and quotations.

## 5. Financial Reporting
Replace estimates with actual costs after purchase. Keep receipts where available and distinguish actual expenditure, quotations, and future projections.
